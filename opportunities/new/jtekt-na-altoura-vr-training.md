## Customer context

JTEKT NA is the North American business of JTEKT Corporation. It operates across automotive systems, bearing solutions, and machine tools. The likely Altoura fit is distributed industrial training across manufacturing sites, operator onboarding, safety procedures, maintenance readiness, and standardised plant-level training.

## Business problem / hypothesis

The submitted request is broad, but the contact title suggests a training-owned evaluation rather than an engineering-only experiment. The most likely drivers are:

- faster onboarding for operators or technicians
- consistent training across facilities
- safer practice for high-risk procedures
- maintenance or troubleshooting simulations
- quality-process training and assessment
- reduction in trainer burden or production disruption during training

## Discovery questions

1. What prompted the VR training inquiry now?
2. Which workforce is the priority: operators, new hires, maintenance technicians, quality inspectors, supervisors, or trainers?
3. Which site, production line, asset, or procedure would make the best first pilot?
4. Are the current materials already available as SOPs, work instructions, CAD/3D assets, photos, or videos?
5. What training metric matters most: time-to-competency, trainer hours, assessment pass rate, safety exposure, quality/rework, or downtime reduction?
6. Who else should be involved: plant operations, EHS, quality, maintenance, HR/L&D, or IT?
7. Do they want VR-only training or a broader Altoura 360 workflow across VR, AR, desktop, mobile, and creator tools?
